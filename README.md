# Order-to-Cash (O2C) Business Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard was developed using the Olist E-commerce dataset to analyze business performance across sales, customer behavior, payment trends, and delivery operations.

The dashboard provides business stakeholders with actionable insights to support operational and strategic decision-making.

---

# Business Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of operational and transactional data. However, without proper visualization and analysis, it becomes difficult to identify:

- Revenue trends
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Delivery performance
- Payment preferences
- High-performing regions and product categories

This project aims to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business intelligence using Power BI.

---

# Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and revenue performance
- Analyze customer payment behavior
- Evaluate delivery efficiency
- Identify top-performing product categories and states
- Create an executive-level dashboard for business decision-making

---

# Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| Total Revenue | Overall sales generated |
| Total Orders | Number of customer orders |
| Average Delivery Time | Average delivery duration |
| On-Time Delivery Rate | Percentage of orders delivered on time |
| Payment Type Analysis | Most used customer payment methods |
| Revenue by State | State-wise revenue distribution |
| Revenue by Category | Product category performance |

# Key Insights

- Credit cards were identified as the dominant payment method.
- Delivery performance maintained a high on-time delivery rate.
- Certain states contributed significantly higher revenue than others.
- Product categories such as Bed, Bath & Table generated strong sales performance.
- Revenue trends revealed seasonal fluctuations in customer purchasing behavior.
